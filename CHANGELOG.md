# Totem Commands

## v1.1.0

Final update: Totem Commands has grown into **Gesture Commands** - the same tap / hold / long-hold for any
spell, item or macro, with a setup window and full controller support. Install it and press **Switch from
Totem Commands** in its window to move your totem button over. Totem Commands keeps working but won't get
new features.

- A one-time chat note on login points to Gesture Commands.

## v1.0.0

First public release.

- Put Call of the Elements on any crossbar slot: tap to cast it, hold for Totemic Recall, keep holding
  for Totemic Call (moves your totems to you). Nothing casts until you release.
- Rumble and on-screen prompts at each hold stage.
- Works on every crossbar bar and page, including a stance bar that takes over a bar.
- `/totemcommands test|on|off|hold <n>|hold2 <n>|debug`.
